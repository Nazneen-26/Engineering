5 Waterproofing Tips Every Homeowner Should Know

Published May 28, 2026

Check your terrace drainage before monsoon

Standing water is the single biggest enemy of any waterproofing membrane. Before monsoon each year, clear terrace drains of debris and confirm water is flowing freely off the surface.

Don't ignore small damp patches

A small damp patch today is often a much bigger repair a year from now. Addressing seepage early, while it's still localized, is far cheaper than waiting for it to spread.

Understand the difference between coatings and membranes

Coatings work for lower-pressure areas like bathroom walls, while membranes are needed wherever there's sustained water pressure, such as basements and terraces. Using the wrong system for the exposure is a common cause of early failure.

Schedule waterproofing before, not during, monsoon

Most systems need dry substrate conditions and several days of curing time. Attempting waterproofing mid-monsoon compromises the bond and shortens the system's lifespan significantly.

Get a flood test before accepting handover

Any reputable waterproofing job should be flood-tested before you sign off — flooding the area and checking for leaks over 24-48 hours confirms the system actually works before it's covered up or tiled over.
