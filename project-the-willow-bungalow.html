The Willow Bungalow

Juhu, Mumbai · Completed Dec 2025

LocationJuhu, Mumbai
Client TypePrivate Homeowner
Work CompletedHome Renovation

The Problem

This heritage-style Juhu bungalow needed a full interior and exterior renovation while preserving its original architectural character — not a modern strip-and-replace job.

Engineering Solution

Our team restored original façade detailing, replaced aging plumbing and electrical, and modernized interiors while retaining the bungalow's period charm, completed exactly on the agreed 8-week schedule.

Materials Used

  • Lime-based heritage plaster
  • Restored teak fittings
  • Copper plumbing lines
  • Weatherproof exterior coating

8 weeks, as originally scheduled.
