Greenfield Society Terrace

Thane · Completed Feb 2026

LocationThane
Client TypeResidential Society
Work CompletedWaterproofing
Completion DateFeb 2026

The Problem

Eight building blocks at Greenfield Society suffered recurring terrace leaks each monsoon despite prior waterproofing attempts, with ceiling seepage reported on multiple top floors.

Engineering Solution

A full slope and drainage survey found inadequate water flow to the outlets. We corrected slope with screed, then applied a UV-stable membrane system across all eight terraces, flood-testing each before handover.

Materials Used

  • APP-modified bitumen membrane
  • Screed concrete for slope correction
  • Protective tile overlay
  • UV-reflective top coat

Completion Timeline

5 weeks across all 8 blocks, sequenced to avoid overlapping disruption.
