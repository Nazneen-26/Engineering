Structural Repair

Restoring full load-bearing capacity to columns, beams and slabs.

Structural Repair addresses the load-bearing skeleton of a building — cracked or spalled columns, sagging beams, and slabs showing rebar corrosion. Left untreated, these issues compound quickly and compromise safety.

Our engineers use rebound hammer and cover-meter testing to quantify the damage before recommending jacketing, micro-concreting, or FRP wrapping — the right fix for the actual condition, not a default one.

Restores original or improved load capacity
Non-destructive testing confirms the real extent of damage
Seismic-grade retrofit options available
Certified corrosion-resistant rebar treatment
Structural stability certificate on completion
