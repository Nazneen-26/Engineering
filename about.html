About Sturdcore Structures

Eighteen years of engineering-led repair, built on trust, transparency, and getting the diagnosis right.

Principal EngineerAr. Vikram Rao
Founder's Story

Built on 18 years of hands-on structural repair experience.

Vikram founded Sturdcore Structures after a decade of consulting on distressed buildings across the region, watching too many owners get patch-fixes instead of real solutions. He set out to build a firm where every crack is diagnosed at its root cause, not just filled in.

A structural engineer by training and a builder at heart, Vikram still personally inspects major projects — pairing technical rigor with a genuine commitment to getting families and businesses back into safe, sound spaces.

"A building doesn't need a cosmetic fix. It needs an engineer who understands why it cracked in the first place." — Vikram Rao, Founder
