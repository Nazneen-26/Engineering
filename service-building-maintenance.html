Building Maintenance

Scheduled AMC plans that catch small issues before they become big ones.

Building Maintenance is our ongoing Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) service — scheduled inspections and minor repairs that catch cracks, seepage, and wear before they escalate into expensive structural work.

Societies and commercial owners on an AMC get a documented inspection report each visit, with any urgent issues flagged immediately rather than surfacing at the next crisis.

Scheduled inspections catch problems early
Documented condition reports each visit
Priority response for urgent issues
Reduces lifetime repair costs significantly
Flexible AMC plans for residential and commercial
